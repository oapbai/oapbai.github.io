Who We Are

Empowering Students Through AI

We're a student-founded organization at Oxbridge Academy dedicated to making artificial intelligence accessible, ethical, and exciting for everyone, regardless of technical background.

Our Mission

Founded in 2026 by David Blackett and Alden Markowski, the Oxbridge Academy AI Club exists to demystify artificial intelligence and empower students to become creators, not just consumers, of technology.

We believe AI literacy is as fundamental as reading and writing in the 21st century. Through hands-on workshops, collaborative projects, and ethical discussions, we prepare our members for a future where AI is ubiquitous.

import torch
import torch.nn as nn

class SimpleNet(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self):
        super().__init__()
        self.layers = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(784, 128),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Linear(128, 10)
        )

    def forward(self, x):
        return self.layers(x)

# Train, evaluate, iterate

What We Do

Activities & Initiatives

From weekly workshops to collaborative projects, our activities are designed to meet you where you are and take you further.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions covering ML fundamentals, neural networks, computer vision, NLP, and more. Beginner-friendly with advanced tracks.

PyTorch · TensorFlow · Jupyter

Build Sprints

Collaborative hackathon-style sessions where teams build real AI applications. From idea to prototype in one afternoon.

Team Coding · Rapid Prototyping · Portfolio

AI Ethics Forum

Structured discussions on bias, privacy, autonomy, and the societal impact of AI.

Ethics · Policy · Debate

Research Reading Group

Paper discussions on cutting-edge ML research. Learn to read, understand, and critique academic papers.

arXiv · Literature Review · Critical Thinking

Peer Mentorship

Experienced members guide newcomers through personalized learning paths, code reviews, and project advice.

1-on-1 · Code Review · Guidance
